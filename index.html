Section 1

Access Your Perks

Every provider runs its own process. This path works for all of them.

  1. 1
    Log in to Foundry

    Perks currently live behind your Foundry login.

  2. 2
    Select the perks banner

    Select Get up to $820,000 in perks at the top of the page. Then select Activate Now to open the Foundry Perks page.

  3. 3
    Filter by what you need

    Sort by Cloud and AI, Finance and Legal, Building and Shipping, Go-to-Market, or Network.

  4. 4
    Read the perk page in full

    Check what you get, the eligibility rules, how to activate, and any provider contact. Some perks, like Microsoft, include extra details on milestones and what happens after approval.

  5. 5
    Activate with the Foundry link or code

    Always start from the link on the perk page. For Microsoft, the web address should include an HBS Foundry ID. If you land on a generic sign-up page, contact support before you continue.

  6. 6
    Give it seven business days

    Some providers send a follow-up form for their formal program. Google usually responds in about seven business days.

  7. 7
    Still waiting? Contact support

    Email hbsfoundrysupport@hbs.edu or use the support form. Tell us which perk, what you tried, and what you saw.

Before you apply, have these ready
  • An email address on your company domain, not Gmail, Yahoo, or Hotmail
  • A live page on that domain. Google may check that something is there.
  • Confirmation you're a new customer of that provider
  • Your incorporation details, for perks like Stripe

Section 2

Get Answers to Common Questions

Eligibility

I've been turned down for perks before. Will this be different?

Likely, yes. Foundry has already vetted the program with providers like AWS, so you aren't applying cold. If an application stalls, contact support and we'll follow up.

I already have a Notion account. Can I still get the perk?

Notion's perk is for new users only. One workaround: open a new Notion workspace on your company domain and move your notes over, by hand or with an AI agent like Claude.

I'm on a lower Google Workspace plan. Can I upgrade to Business Plus through the perk?

It's up to Google. Google Cloud and Google Workspace are run by separate teams, so approval from one doesn't guarantee the other. Submit the Google form from the perk page. Then contact Google's startup support, explain you've joined Foundry, and ask for the upgrade.

I already set up Google Cloud or AWS for my MVP. Did I miss out?

Not necessarily. For Google Cloud, apply through the perk page and watch for an invitation to Google's formal program. For AWS, new-user rules may apply, so submit the application and see. If you hear nothing in seven business days, contact support.

Do I need a business email?

For most perks, yes. Providers flag Gmail, Yahoo, and Hotmail addresses as personal. An address on your own domain (you@yourcompany.com) reads as a work email and often passes verification.

Do I need a website, or is a domain enough?

Google is strict and may check that something lives at your domain. No site yet? Use the Lovable perk to put up a simple "coming soon" page in about an hour.

Timing and Access

Is there a deadline to apply?

No. Alumni keep access, for now indefinitely. A provider can leave the program, but that's rare. Once you activate a perk, the provider's own time limit starts.

How will I find perks after Bootcamp ends?

We'll email you once the standalone perks space is live. Until then, email support, say you're a Foundry alum, and ask for the perks list and links.

Can I use more than one cloud provider?

Yes. You don't have to commit to one. Apply to Google Cloud, Microsoft, and AWS, test all three, and keep what works.

Getting Help

The Microsoft link sends me to a generic page, or says it can't verify my company. What now?

Check the web address. It should include an HBS Foundry ID. If it doesn't, or verification fails, email support with a screenshot. We've flagged this with Microsoft.

I emailed a provider's dedicated contact and haven't heard back.

Some contacts support thousands of founders, so replies can take time. If you're blocked, copy support on your follow-up so we can help.

Is there a full list of perks and what each one includes?

Yes. The Foundry Perks page lists every perk with its benefits, eligibility, activation steps, and provider contacts.

Is there a perks-specific form?

Use the support form or email hbsfoundrysupport@hbs.edu. Both reach the team that handles perks.

Beyond the List

Can I suggest a perk?

Please do. Four or five of our live perks started as founder suggestions. Email support with the company and why it would help founders.

Can my company become a Foundry perk?

Email support with what your company offers and why it would help Bootcamp founders. Harvard will review it. You can also share it with your peers on the community page.

How much GitHub credit comes with the Microsoft perk?

Check the Microsoft perk page. It's likely a standard, modest amount. A standalone GitHub perk is in the works and should offer more.

Is my data safe with these cloud providers?

AWS, Google, and Microsoft each publish privacy and security terms, and they commit not to train on your data. Their business depends on that trust. Still, no one can guarantee the future. If you handle sensitive data, read each provider's terms closely and weigh the risk against the speed you gain.

Google Workspace or Microsoft for company email?

From Dany's experience running a startup: Google Workspace had better support, simpler integrations, and lower cost, and she found Gemini more useful than Copilot. You can add as many addresses as you need from the Google Admin console, such as info@ and a personal address for each founder.

Section 3

Use These Hacks

Timing matters. Some perks pay off right away. Others expire before you can use them if you activate too early.

Activate now

HubSpot: 90% off your first year, plus direct support from HubSpot staff. Traction is customers, so build your pipeline early. Notion: six months free, and far cheaper than an enterprise suite.

When you're ready to show

Lovable: build an MVP or website fast. The perk lasts about two months, so start when you're ready to put something in front of customers or investors.

When you incorporate

Stripe: 50% off your first year. Incorporating and setting up a company domain also unlocks many other perks.

When you have an MVP

Datadog: two years of monitoring to see how users move through your product. Without a product, it's wasted.

When you'll use compute

AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure: wait until you'll actually burn credits.

When you fundraise

Carta: built for fundraising. Save it for your raise.

Stretch Your Perks Further

  • Apply to all three clouds. Collect Google, Microsoft, and AWS credits, then test them side by side.
  • Pick a cloud by goal. Google Cloud has the highest ceiling for AI builders. AWS credits arrive fastest. Search "Microsoft for Startups milestones" to see each tier.
  • Get your domain first. A company domain and email unlock most perks. Founders in the session also recommended Zoho Mail for free or low-cost email on your domain.
  • Put a page on your domain in an hour. Use Lovable for a "coming soon" page, and deploy free on Vercel. Already have a site? Ask Lovable or Claude to recreate it.
  • Treat HubSpot support as a free consultant. You get a HubSpot specialist for a year, which is expensive to buy on your own.
  • Deep tech? Look at MathWorks. Engineering and AI toolkits used by teams like NASA and Tesla, plus training on AI agents and MCPs.
  • Come back as an alum. Activate perks when they fit your stage, even after Bootcamp ends.
