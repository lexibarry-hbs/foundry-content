Section 1

Review the Key Takeaways

  • Catalyst is a separate, selective program with its own application. Bootcamp doesn't guarantee a spot, and applying is optional.
  • The value is the experience: four weeks of hands-on work with experts, mentors, and other committed founders. Most participants won't win prize money.
  • If you won't subscribe to Foundry after Bootcamp, apply by the December 1, 2026 priority deadline. Your Bootcamp access ends December 2, and applying first lets you keep updating your application.
  • Applying early doesn't change how you're evaluated. You can keep improving your application until the February 1, 2027 final deadline, and the latest version is what counts.
  • You'll submit a questionnaire, your strongest pitch deck, and a 90-second pitch video. Spend real time on the video. If you can pitch in 90 seconds, you can pitch to anyone.
  • Catalyst is now open to ventures in any sector. The first round focused on deep tech.

Section 2

Understand What Catalyst Is

  1. 1
    Apply

    Submit one application per venture through your Foundry workspace. The first round drew 500+ applications.

  2. 2
    Join Catalyst

    About 25 ventures are selected for four weeks of hands-on work: two to three live sessions with industry experts, plus pairings with mentors and experts (real people, not AI).

  3. 3
    Pitch at Demo Day

    All Catalyst founders pitch at an end-of-program showcase. The strongest five to six founders are selected for the pitch event.

  4. 4
    Compete at the pitch event

    Finalists pitch in person on the Harvard Business School campus for a non-dilutive top prize, with runner-up prizes too. Foundry covers travel and accommodation for finalists.

Virtual program, in-person finale

The program itself is virtual on Zoom, like Bootcamp. Only the pitch event is in person. In the first round, one founder won $100,000 (as shared in the session; check the Catalyst FAQ for this round's prizes).

Section 3

Check Your Eligibility

You Can Apply If

  • You have a Foundry account.
  • You and your venture are based in the U.S.
  • You're a founder or cofounder of the venture.
  • Your venture's lifetime revenue is under $500,000. Zero revenue is fine.
  • Your venture has raised $1 million or less in external funding.
  • You can attend the live sessions, scheduled expert and mentor office hours, Demo Day, and the pitch event if selected.

Not Required

  • Finishing the Bootcamp curriculum. The team still recommends it, because the final module builds the pitch you'll need for the application.
  • An active Foundry subscription.
  • An application fee. There's no fee to apply.

Section 4

Find and Build Your Application

  1. 1
    Log in to Foundry

    Open your Foundry workspace home page.

  2. 2
    Find the Catalyst banner

    Look on the right side of the page. Scroll down if you don't see it at the top.

  3. 3
    Submit one application per venture

    Coordinate with your cofounders so only one of you applies. If a venture submits twice, reviewers look at only one.

  4. 4
    Keep improving it

    Update your answers, deck, and video as your venture grows, up to the February 1, 2027 final deadline.

Prepare What You'll Submit

A questionnaire

Questions about your venture, you, and your team, similar in format to the Bootcamp application.

Your strongest pitch deck

It can be the one you build with Foundry AI, but it doesn't have to be.

A 90-second pitch video

Show that you know your problem, your solution, and your users. Practice until you have it down.

Show Reviewers You're Ready

Founder readiness

Your commitment, how coachable you are, and what you've learned and accomplished so far.

Venture readiness

A strong, defensible opportunity, real customer discovery and validation, market potential, and traction or momentum.

A plan forward

The milestones you're tracking and how Catalyst will help you reach them.

No minimums

There's no minimum revenue, user count, or market size. Matt described the review as holistic: the team reads every answer, watches every video, and reviews every deck. To show traction at your stage, revisit the traction content in your Bootcamp and the November 16 Pitch Session with Mercury.

Section 5

Know the Key Dates

Now

Applications are open in your Foundry workspace. Start any time.

December 1, 2026

Priority deadline for your Bootcamp cohort. Check your Bootcamp Hub for your cohort's dates.

December 2, 2026

Last day of Bootcamp access if you don't subscribe. To apply after this, you'll need a Foundry subscription. There are no deadline extensions.

February 1, 2027

Final deadline. Reviews happen after this date, and the latest version of your application is what's considered.

Spring 2027

The four-week Catalyst program runs, likely in May (dates still being finalized), followed by Demo Day and the pitch event.

Why the priority deadline matters

If you apply by December 1, you can still log in after Bootcamp ends and update your application without a subscription. You'll see only your Catalyst application, not the rest of your workspace. The holidays are also busy, so getting an application in now means you can come back to it in January.

Section 6

Decide If Now Is the Right Time

Ask Yourself

  • Can you point to concrete traction for your stage?
  • Can you explain the urgent problem you're solving in 90 seconds, on camera?
  • Do you know how Catalyst will help you unlock your next milestone?
  • Can you commit to the attendance and engagement the program asks for in spring 2027?

What Catalyst Asks of You

  • Show up. Attendance at live sessions and scheduled office hours is mandatory.
  • Support your community. Expect to give and get peer feedback from other founders.
  • Make real decisions. Expect hard feedback and pressure testing of you, your venture, and your solution.
It's OK to wait

If the timing isn't right, that's OK. You can apply in a future round. Working on your application, especially your 90-second pitch, is valuable no matter what you decide.

Section 7

Get Answers to Founder Questions

Eligibility

Does my venture need operations in the U.S., or is a U.S. corporation or LLC enough?

Matt's guidance: your venture should have operations in the U.S. as well as being incorporated here. You can still serve customers outside the U.S.

My company is U.S.-based, but my cofounder lives outside the U.S. Is that a problem?

It shouldn't be, as long as at least one founder is based in the U.S.

Can I apply if my venture has no revenue yet?

Yes. Other evidence of traction counts, such as a letter of intent or a granted patent.

My company isn't registered in the U.S. yet, but I'll be here for the whole program. Do I qualify?

This depends on your situation. Email the Foundry team so they can look at your specifics.

I'm an international student on a visa. Do I need work authorization to take part?

Matt thought probably not, but he couldn't give a definite answer. Check with an immigration attorney or your school's international office. This recap isn't legal advice.

Who else is applying to this round?

Anyone who has taken part in Bootcamp since Foundry launched in April 2026 can apply, including the Bootcamp cohort after yours. Multiple Bootcamp cohorts apply to each Catalyst round.

Focus and Fit

Is Catalyst focused on a particular sector?

No. The first round focused on deep tech. This round is open to ventures in any sector, and there's no theme.

All six finalists last round came out of academia. Is that a bias?

Matt explained that the first round focused on deep tech, and science-based ventures often come out of universities and labs. This round is more open.

What does “scalable” mean for Catalyst?

It's about how big your venture could become, not your current traction. Traction matters too, but reviewers look at it separately.

Is traction the most important thing reviewers look at?

There isn't one most important metric. The review is holistic, and reviewers account for your stage and industry.

Is there a minimum market size (TAM)?

No. There's no minimum TAM.

Is Catalyst only for ventures that will raise venture capital?

No. Many past Catalyst ventures will likely raise venture capital eventually, but many start with grants such as SBIR or STTR first. Catalyst can help both.

Your Application and Video

Where do I find the application?

In your Foundry workspace, on the right side of the home page. Scroll down if you don't see it. If you still can't find it, email the Foundry team.

Should my 90-second video be just me talking, or can I add slides or images?

Matt recommends keeping it to you on camera, like an elevator pitch, since you'll submit your deck separately. Visuals won't necessarily hurt you, but reviewers want to see how you pitch, not you relying on a script or slides.

Can my cofounder appear in the video?

Yes, if it better captures your venture and fits the video limits. Lexi noted that two speakers in 90 seconds may or may not serve your pitch, so think about what works for your story.

Do I have to record my video in Foundry?

No. Record it however works best, as long as the file uploads to your Catalyst application.

When will reviewers look at my application?

Regina explained that reviews start after the February 1 final deadline and the latest version you submit is what's considered. Matt's advice: don't submit a placeholder. Treat every version you submit as one a reviewer could see.

If I update my video after a pilot, can I flag that it's a real change?

There's no way to flag it, but reviewers look at everything you submit and will see where your venture stands. Your latest video is the one that counts.

Can you show how the Foundry AI pitch deck works?

The pitch module at the end of Bootcamp walks you through the pitch simulator, deck generation, and recording your video. If you need more help, email the Foundry team. They may also host a session on pitching.

Deadlines and Access

Do I have to start before December 2 and submit by February 1?

You can start and submit any time. If you don't plan to subscribe, apply by December 1 while you still have access. After December 2, you'll need a subscription to apply.

If I apply before the priority deadline and update afterward, am I still a priority applicant?

The priority deadline doesn't affect how you're evaluated. It's about keeping access to your application so you can update it.

If I apply by December 1 but don't subscribe, can I still update my application?

Yes. You'll be able to log in and edit your Catalyst application. To see the rest of your workspace, including Foundry AI agents and tools, you'll need to subscribe.

Is February 2027 the last chance for my cohort? What about future rounds?

February 1, 2027 is the final deadline for this round. Matt believes you can apply to future rounds too. The team will confirm.

When does the four-week program start?

Spring 2027, likely in May. Dates are still being finalized.

The Program and Pitch Event

Is Catalyst in person?

No. Catalyst is virtual on Zoom, like Bootcamp. You only need to travel for the pitch event on the Harvard Business School campus if you're selected, and Foundry covers travel and accommodation.

Are the mentors and experts real people?

Yes.

How long is the pitch at the pitch event?

At the first pitch event, finalists had seven minutes. Check the Catalyst FAQ for this round. The application video is 90 seconds.

Can I attend the pitch event to support other founders?

Not currently. It's a closed, private event, and even Catalyst founders who didn't pitch couldn't attend. The team hopes to open it up as the program grows.

Can I watch past Catalyst pitches?

Not at this time. Sharing pitches requires each founder's permission.

What's a good next step if I don't get into Catalyst?

Matt suggested applying again, continuing to build on the Foundry platform, and considering other accelerators and incubators.

Your Foundry Subscription

How much is the Foundry subscription after Bootcamp?

$49 a month, as shared in the session. Check the platform for current pricing. The fee is for Foundry. Catalyst itself has no fee.

How do I subscribe, and for how long?

You'll get an invitation to subscribe in the platform at the end of Bootcamp. You can keep access as long as you subscribe.

Can I restart my subscription after it lapses?

Yes.
