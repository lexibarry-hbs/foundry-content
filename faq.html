What is Foundry Bootcamp?

Foundry Bootcamp is an eight-week program for founders. Use the calendar to follow the program themes and plan for optional live sessions.

Are live sessions required?

Live sessions are optional, but highly encouraged. They are designed to help founders apply the week’s ideas and connect with the Foundry community.

Where do I find session links?

Session links and updates are shared through the Community Platform. Check there for the most current access information before each session.

Are live sessions recorded?

Live sessions are recorded and distributed weekly, except for Community Sessions. Visit the Content Library to find available recaps and recordings.

Can the schedule change?

Yes. The schedule is subject to change. Use the Community Platform for the latest updates and additional events.

Where can I revisit a session?

Visit the Content Library for session recaps, recordings, founder questions, and practical next steps.

Who should I contact with questions?

Email hbsfoundrysupport@hbs.edu if you have a question about a session or resource.
